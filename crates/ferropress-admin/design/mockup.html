Welcome to Ferropress

Ferropress keeps WordPress's authoring soul and modernizes the delivery: pages are pre-rendered to static HTML and regenerated only when the content that feeds them changes.

How it presses

You compose here, on the sheet. When you save, the block tree is written to the store and the affected pages regenerate off the change feed — no full rebuild.

Keep the soul. Modernize the press.

The dynamic parts — search, live comments — mount as rinch islands on the otherwise static page

  • Static-first, regenerated on change
  • Rust all the way down
  • Capability-sandboxed plugins
